The Innovation Hub

The SAE ZHCET Collegiate Club is AMU's flagship high-performance motorsport engineering division. Here, complex automotive theory transforms into race-ready machines competing at national Formula Student tracks.

From custom military-spec wire looms and CAN-Bus telemetry logging to aerodynamic wing packages, our students design, manufacture, and test high-velocity prototypes.

Engineering The Prototype

01 // POWERTRAIN

High-Rev Engine

Optimized race-tuned powertrain with custom CNC-machined intake runner, titanium exhaust header, and aggressive fuel map curves delivering 13,500 RPM.

02 // AERODYNAMICS

Carbon Aero Package

Dual-element carbon fiber front wing, underbody ground effect venturi tunnel, and adjustable rear wing delivering over 145 kg of downforce.

03 // SUSPENSION

Double Wishbone

Direct pushrod-actuated adjustable dampers paired with anti-roll bar geometry engineered for razor-sharp transient response on apexes.

04 // TELEMETRY

Custom PCB telemetry boards recording live wheel-speed sensors, suspension potentiometers, and real-time wireless pit-wall transmission.
